## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a dependable, academic yet thoroughly modern ethos tailored for collegiate and early-career professional development. Its visual narrative bridges institutional rigor with accessible, straightforward utility. By discarding transient web trends—such as decorative gradient meshes, intense glassmorphism, or aggressive startup typography—the system projects calm competence, clarity, and structural focus.

Key Brand Pillars:
- **Institutional Reliability**: Grounded in deep collegiate blues and neutral slate surfaces, the UI mirrors the authority of premier academic portals and modern workflow tools.
- **Cognitive Clarity**: Prioritizes low cognitive load during high-stress activities (e.g., crafting a first resume or selecting career pathways). Generous breathing room, structural borders, and clean typographic hierarchies guide the student effortlessly.
- **Utilitarian Elegance**: Visual interest stems from impeccable proportions, disciplined 8pt grid metrics, crisp 1px borders, and nuanced tone-on-tone elevation rather than ornamental flair.

## Colors

The color palette centers on a disciplined collegiate palette constructed to ensure maximum contrast and clear semantic priority.

- **Primary (`#1E3A8A`)**: Deep Collegiate Navy. Serves as the key anchor for brand identity, top-level navigation bars, primary active states, and structural badges.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`)**: Royal Blue. Applied to primary interactive elements, interactive link highlights, focus rings, and primary calls to action.
- **Tertiary (`#0D9488`)**: Deep Teal. Reserved for positive progress indicators, resume score optimizations, and career milestone tracking.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`)**: Slate 900. Dictates high-contrast text hierarchies, headings, and terminal interface components.

### Surface Tiers & Borders
- **Canvas Base**: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) provides an off-white background that reduces ocular fatigue during sustained sessions.
- **Surface Elevation**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) distinguishes content modules, resume canvas previews, and editable containers.
- **Subtle Surface**: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) handles nested cards, code blocks, and table headers.
- **Structural Border**: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) strictly enforces component boundaries with a standardized 1px hairline stroke.
- **Muted Text**: `#475569` (Slate 600) ensures accessible, high-contrast secondary reading material (surpassing WCAG AAA requirements).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes `Inter` exclusively to maintain systematic legibility across complex multi-column resume builders, career roadmaps, and data dense assessment sheets.

- **Headings**: Formulated with negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.025em`) and semi-bold weights to produce architectural presence without shouting.
- **Body**: Uses neutral tracking and tall line-heights (minimum 1.5 ratio) to ensure effortless scanning of employment histories, educational records, and job criteria.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Medium (`500`) to Semi-bold (`600`) weights preserve visual definition on functional controls, form field headers, tags, and status chips.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system adheres to a strict 8pt rhythm system (with 4pt sub-increments for compact inputs and badges).

### Grid Infrastructure
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column grid, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and up to `2rem` (32px) outer margins, capped at a maximum content width of `1280px` to maintain optimal line lengths.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column layout, `1.5rem` gutters, `1.5rem` margins. Resume preview reflows below or into a split-pane collapsible view.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout, `1rem` gutters, `1rem` margins. Complex multi-column forms collapse to linear vertical stacks.

### Spacing Philosophy
Interfaces must prioritize whitespace around section headers and group controls into visually discrete clusters using `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px) internally, separated by `space-lg` (24px) or `space-xl` (32px) to clearly define relationships.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is predominantly articulated through **tonal separation** and **hairline outlines**, supplemented with minimal ambient shadows to denote lift and floating interactions.

1. **Flat / Base Layer**:
   - `0px` elevation: Background canvas (`#F8FAFC`) directly bounds layout modules.
2. **Structural Layer (Default Cards)**:
   - Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`
   - Shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
   - Used for cards, static form groupings, career path modules, and resume sections.
3. **Interactive & Hover Layer**:
   - Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`
   - Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`
   - Used for clickable roadmap cards, active form fields, and dropdown selections.
4. **Floating / Overlay Layer**:
   - Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`
   - Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
   - Used for modal dialogs, career assessment drawers, autocomplete menus, and contextual tooltips.

## Shapes

The design system implements a controlled `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem / 8px baseline radius), imparting a contemporary, tailored, and approachable tone while preserving structural formality.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Standard for inputs, buttons, select triggers, badges, and small containers.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / 16px)**: Applied to main workflow cards, resume preview panels, and guidance module blocks.
- **Extra Large Radius (`1.5rem` / 24px)**: Reserved for modal containers and full-screen guidance drawers.
- **Full Radius (`9999px`)**: Strictly reserved for status chips, step completion indicators, and user avatar wrappers.
